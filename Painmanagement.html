UNDERSTANDING PAIN MANAGEMENT

What is Pain Management?

Pain Management in physiotherapy involves a structured approach to understanding symptoms, improving movement and supporting functional recovery. Treatment is based on individual assessment and clinical requirements.

01

Understand Your Pain

Your physiotherapist discusses your symptoms, activities, movement patterns and functional concerns to better understand your condition.

02

Targeted Treatment

Appropriate physiotherapy techniques may be selected according to your assessment and individual treatment goals.

03

Improve Function

Rehabilitation may focus on movement, strength, flexibility and confidence with everyday activities.

OUR APPROACH

Pain Management Approaches

Depending on your assessment, your physiotherapist may combine different rehabilitation strategies to support your recovery.

01

Exercise Therapy

Carefully selected exercises may help support strength, mobility and functional movement.

02

Manual Therapy

Appropriate hands-on techniques may be included as part of an individualised rehabilitation plan.

03

Movement Education

Guidance may help you understand movement and gradually return to meaningful activities.

04

Mobility Training

Suitable mobility exercises may be used to support comfortable and functional movement.

05

Strength Training

Progressive strengthening may be incorporated according to your abilities and goals.

06

Recovery Education

Your physiotherapist may provide practical guidance to support self-management and recovery.
