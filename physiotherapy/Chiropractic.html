UNDERSTANDING THE THERAPY

What is Chiropractic Care?

Chiropractic care focuses on the assessment and management of musculoskeletal conditions and movement-related concerns. The specific approach depends on an individual's assessment and needs.

01

Assessment First

Your practitioner considers your symptoms, movement, medical history and functional requirements before recommending treatment.

02

Individualised Approach

Treatment is selected according to your presentation, comfort, movement limitations and rehabilitation goals.

03

Functional Focus

The wider goal may include supporting mobility, movement confidence and everyday function.

OUR APPROACH

Chiropractic Care Approaches

Depending on your assessment, different approaches may be incorporated into your individualised care programme.

01

Spinal Mobility

Appropriate techniques may be used to support comfortable spinal movement and mobility.

02

Joint Movement

Treatment may focus on selected areas where movement and function require professional attention.

03

Soft Tissue Care

Suitable soft tissue techniques may be incorporated as part of a broader rehabilitation programme.

04

Postural Guidance

Guidance may help you understand movement and posture during everyday activities.

05

Exercise Support

Exercises may be recommended to complement hands-on treatment and encourage active rehabilitation.

06

Movement Education

Education can help you understand movement strategies relevant to your daily activities.
