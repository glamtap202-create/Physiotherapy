MANUAL THERAPY

Helping You Move With Greater Confidence

Manual Therapy is a hands-on approach that may be included as part of an individualised physiotherapy programme. Techniques are selected according to your assessment, symptoms and functional goals.

Your physiotherapist will assess your movement and determine which techniques may be appropriate alongside exercises and other rehabilitation strategies.

UNDERSTANDING THE THERAPY

What is Manual Therapy?

Manual Therapy involves hands-on techniques performed by a trained physiotherapy professional. The specific technique and treatment approach depend on your assessment and clinical requirements.

01

Assessment First

Your physiotherapist evaluates your symptoms, movement and functional requirements before selecting an appropriate treatment approach.

02

Targeted Techniques

Hands-on techniques may be selected according to the area being treated and your individual rehabilitation goals.

03

Functional Recovery

Manual therapy can be incorporated into a wider programme focused on movement, exercise and functional rehabilitation.

OUR APPROACH

Manual Therapy Techniques

Depending on your assessment, your physiotherapist may select appropriate hands-on techniques as part of your rehabilitation programme.

01

Soft Tissue Techniques

Hands-on techniques may be used to address soft tissue areas and support comfortable movement.

02

Joint Mobilisation

Appropriate mobilisation techniques may be considered to support joint movement and function.

03

Movement Techniques

Hands-on guidance may be combined with movement strategies to improve functional performance.

04

Stretching Techniques

Suitable assisted stretching may be incorporated according to your individual clinical requirements.

05

Myofascial Techniques

Selected hands-on approaches may be used as one component of an individualised treatment plan.

06

Assisted Movement

Your therapist may guide movement during treatment based on your functional needs.

WHY IT MAY BE USED

Manual Therapy is not a one-size-fits-all treatment. Your physiotherapist determines whether hands-on techniques are suitable for your individual needs.

01

Movement Support

02

Individualised Care

03

Rehabilitation Support

04
