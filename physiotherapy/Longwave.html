LONGWAVE THERAPY

Supporting Your Rehabilitation Journey

Longwave Therapy is a modality that can be considered as part of a broader physiotherapy programme. Treatment selection is based on individual assessment, symptoms, functional goals and clinical requirements.

Your physiotherapist can determine whether this modality is appropriate for you and how it should be combined with other rehabilitation techniques.

UNDERSTANDING THE THERAPY

What is Longwave Therapy?

Longwave Therapy refers to the use of electromagnetic energy within a therapeutic setting. The exact application, intensity and treatment parameters should be selected by a qualified healthcare professional.

01

Assessment First

Your physiotherapist assesses your condition, symptoms and functional requirements before selecting an appropriate treatment approach.

02

Individualised Application

Treatment parameters can be selected according to the individual's clinical presentation and rehabilitation goals.

03

Combined Rehabilitation

Where appropriate, a modality may be combined with exercise, manual therapy or other physiotherapy interventions.

WHY IT MAY BE USED

Part of a Complete Approach

The suitability and potential role of Longwave Therapy depends on your individual assessment. Your physiotherapist will guide you through the most appropriate treatment plan.

01

Comfort-Focused Care

Treatment can be incorporated into a comfortable and supervised physiotherapy session where clinically appropriate.

02

Clinical Planning

Treatment selection is guided by assessment rather than using the same approach for every patient.

03

Rehabilitation Support

It may be used as one component of a broader physiotherapy and rehabilitation programme.

04

Professional Supervision

A trained physiotherapy professional should determine whether the treatment is suitable.
