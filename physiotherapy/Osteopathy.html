UNDERSTANDING THE THERAPY

What is Osteopathic Rehabilitation?

Osteopathic rehabilitation takes a whole-person approach to movement and function. The treatment plan is adapted according to the individual's clinical presentation and goals.

01

Movement Assessment

Your physiotherapist reviews your movement, mobility and functional concerns to understand your individual needs.

02

Individualised Approach

Treatment strategies are selected according to your symptoms, assessment findings and rehabilitation goals.

03

Functional Recovery

The wider programme may focus on mobility, strength, movement confidence and everyday functional activities.

OUR APPROACH

Osteopathic Treatment Approach

Depending on your assessment, your physiotherapist may use appropriate movement and hands-on strategies as part of your rehabilitation programme.

01

02

03

Postural Guidance

04

05

06

Rehabilitation exercises may be included to support strength, mobility and functional goals.
