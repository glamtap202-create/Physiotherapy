UNDERSTANDING THE THERAPY

What is Laser Therapy?

Laser Therapy uses light energy in a therapeutic setting. The type of laser, treatment parameters and clinical application should be selected by a suitably trained healthcare professional according to the patient's assessment.

01

Clinical Assessment

Your physiotherapist considers your symptoms, condition, functional requirements and rehabilitation goals before treatment.

02

Individualised Treatment

Treatment parameters are selected according to the individual's clinical presentation and treatment requirements.

03

Integrated Care

Where appropriate, laser therapy may be used alongside exercise therapy, manual therapy or other rehabilitation approaches.

WHY IT MAY BE USED

Part of a Complete Approach

The role of Laser Therapy depends on your individual assessment. Your physiotherapist will determine whether it is appropriate and how it may fit into your rehabilitation programme.

01

Targeted Application

The treatment can be applied according to the area and clinical requirements identified during assessment.

02

Individualised Planning

Treatment selection is based on individual needs rather than using the same approach for every patient.

03

Rehabilitation Support

Laser therapy may form one component of a broader physiotherapy programme.

04

Professional Guidance

A trained physiotherapy professional should determine whether this modality is suitable for you.

IMPORTANT INFORMATION

Treatment Starts With Assessment

Laser Therapy may not be appropriate for every person or every condition. Your physiotherapist will consider your clinical history, symptoms, treatment goals and any relevant precautions before recommending treatment.

Please inform your physiotherapist about relevant health information, medications or concerns before treatment.
