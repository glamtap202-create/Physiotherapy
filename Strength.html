STRENGTH TRAINING

Build Strength For Better Movement

Strength training can form an important part of a physiotherapy and rehabilitation programme. Exercises are selected according to your movement, strength levels and individual goals.

Your physiotherapist can guide you through appropriate strengthening exercises and progress them as your ability improves.

UNDERSTANDING THE THERAPY

What is Strength Training?

Strength training uses controlled resistance exercises to develop or maintain muscle strength and support physical function. In physiotherapy, the programme is adapted to the individual's needs.

01

Assessment First

Your physiotherapist considers your current strength, movement and functional abilities before selecting exercises.

02

Controlled Exercise

Exercises are performed with appropriate technique, control and resistance according to your programme.

03

Progressive Recovery

Training can be progressed gradually as your strength, control and confidence develop.

OUR APPROACH

Strength Training Areas

Depending on your assessment, your rehabilitation programme may include different forms of strengthening and functional training.

01

Lower Body Strength

Exercises may focus on the muscles involved in standing, walking, stairs and other lower-limb activities.

02

Upper Body Strength

Targeted exercises may support strength required for reaching, lifting and everyday upper-limb activities.

03

Core Strength

Appropriate exercises may help develop trunk control and support functional movement.

04

Resistance Training

Resistance may be provided using body weight, bands, weights or other suitable exercise equipment.

05

Functional Training

Exercises can be selected around movements that are important for your daily activities.

06

Balance & Control

Strength exercises may be combined with balance and movement-control activities.

WHY IT MAY BE USED

Training For Functional Recovery

Strength training is individualised and can be adapted according to your condition, exercise tolerance and rehabilitation goals.

01

Muscle Strength

Progressive exercise may help improve and maintain muscle strength.

02

Movement Confidence

Developing strength can support confidence during appropriate functional activities.

03

Functional Performance

Exercises can be selected to support activities relevant to your daily life.

04

Progressive Rehabilitation

Training can be modified as your physical capacity changes during recovery.
