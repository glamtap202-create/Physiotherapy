WHAT IS Lumbar Spondylosis?

↓

Lumbar spondylosis is a degenerative condition affecting the lower back, caused by age-related wear and tear on the spinal discs and joints. It can lead to pain, stiffness, and reduced mobility in the lumbar spine.

WHAT ARE THE SYMPTOMS OF LUMBAR SPONDYLOSIS?

↓

The symptoms vary from person to person, some of them include:

  • Lower back pain and stiffness, especially in the morning.
  • Pain radiating to the hips, buttocks, or legs.
  • Reduced flexibility in the lower back.
  • Muscle weakness or numbness in the legs.
  • Discomfort that worsens with prolonged sitting or standing.
